Projects

  • SAR Brain

    A reinforcement learning environment where agents replay medicinal chemistry campaigns and choose which compounds to predict, make, and test.

  • Molarium

    Edit a molecule in its protein binding pocket and simulate how it moves, all in the browser.

  • Foldarium Beta

    Tests whether chemists can recognize an accurate ligand pose that a co-folding model ranked too low.

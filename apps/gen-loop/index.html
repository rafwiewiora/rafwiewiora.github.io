1
Design Strategy
Human + AI
The human expert defines the design objectives and which molecular vectors to explore. The AI team lead translates these into reward functions, hard gates, and structural filters for the generative model.
2
Generate Molecules
GPU Cluster
REINVENT runs reinforcement learning on a GPU cluster, generating large batches of candidates each iteration under multi-objective optimization, constrained by structural filters and property gates.
3
Predict Properties
ML Models
Every generated molecule is scored by ML models across several ADME and potency axes. Multi-parameter optimization keeps the compounds that clear most of the objectives at once.
4
Dock & Validate
3D Structure
Top-scoring compounds are docked into the protein binding site and checked for pose quality, strain, and fit to the target pharmacophore.
5
Expert Review
Human
The medicinal chemist reviews compounds in 3D — binding pose, synthetic accessibility, and SAR — curates selections, and refines the rules based on what the model is learning to make.
6
Learn & Iterate
Closed loop
Each iteration’s failures are diagnosed and turned into concrete design changes, and the model warm-starts from the previous checkpoint, carrying forward what it learned.
Iterative refinement, not one-shot prediction

Across many design cycles, the system learns where simple scoring breaks down, when to promote a soft objective to a hard constraint, and which properties the scaffold itself locks in. Each lesson is found through failure, diagnosed with the chemist, and encoded as a rule for future iterations.

🧑‍🔬 Human Expert

  • Defines design objectives and binding hypotheses
  • Sets go/no-go decisions on scoring strategy
  • Reviews 3D poses and applies medicinal-chemistry judgment
  • Diagnoses model failures and proposes design changes
  • Curates final compound selections

🤖 AI Agent Team

  • Generates and scores molecules autonomously, around the clock
  • Runs property predictions on every compound
  • Manages docking and pose validation
  • Assembles curated selections for review
  • Maintains a decision log and lab notebook

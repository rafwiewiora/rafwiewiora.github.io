Coverage and cost assumptions

Each square represents a measured patent example. Platforms can share compounds and prepared intermediates.

Costs are equal-weight preparation operations from the declared starting stock. Making a compound retains sufficient quantities of every intermediate produced on its recorded route. This retained-material assumption enables cross-platform savings. Quotes stop at any available intermediate; alternative routes are not re-optimized. A multi-operation source record remains one recorded transformation with its original cost.

The large retrosyntheses follow the main substrate chain. Separately prepared co-reactants are pictured beside the arrows, with their full recorded preparations in the “Co-reactant preparation” section at the bottom of that column. All their costs are included. Observed compounds are not a complete factorial grid. Activities are IC50 in nM, not binding Kd.

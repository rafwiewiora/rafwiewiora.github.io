Both strategies had the bromide

In one representative replay, the fixed-price rule prepared Stage 174.1 by batch 4 and waited until batch 18 to make Example 175. The updating rule prepared Stage 174.1 by batch 9 and chose Example 175 in batch 10. The winning batches finished at 133 and 68 operations, respectively. This paired start was selected for its savings near the median. The sequence is simulated.

Across 128 paired starts

With the original quotes, the median cost to the 0.5 nM winner was 133 operations. With updating, it was 60. The updating rule reached the winner sooner in 109 starts, tied in 6, and took longer in 13. The 128 paired differences averaged 59.1 operations saved.

What if every local FEP prediction were exact?

At a native OpenFE/LoMap score of at least 0.90, Example 175 has just one FEP neighbour among these 79 compounds. Neither Example 174 nor Example 176 passes that threshold. Requiring every proposed compound to be adjacent to a made reference reaches the winner in 9 of 128 starts, including 6 starts that already contain it.

Allow the SAR model to propose compounds anywhere, then replace its predictions with perfect affinities wherever a made neighbour allows FEP. On those same 128 starts, median cost with updating falls from 60 to 34 operations. The hybrid reaches the winner in every start with a median of 15 virtual queries. This benchmark uses measured affinities as ideal predictions and assigns zero cost to prediction.

Replay assumptions

Three randomly chosen starting compounds, paid from the recorded synthesis frontier. Seed sets 0–63 and 1000–1063. Frozen batches of four. SAR uses a similarity-weighted average of up to five measured neighbours, plus a novelty term. Price-sensitive rankings subtract 0.5 × ln(max(1, synthesis operations)). Exact plans retain all prepared molecules, and assays become available after each completed batch. One recorded preparative operation costs one unit. The model omits quantities, yields, elapsed time, assays and preparation before the available source records.

Starting stock and model boundaries

Every panel starts with the same declared pool: core acid chloride and dichloro core ester, ready standard head blocks, amino benzyl alcohol, o-toluidine, head-diversification amines and tail amines. No route intermediates are pre-made. Setup is charged when first used.

Intermediate preparation is reused with enough material retained. Costs count reaction stages, excluding extra building-block preparation, finishing, yields and batch scale. Click a prepared intermediate to outline the grid region it helps.

The three grids are schematic choices, not an experimentally measured 3 × 3 panel. Structures are generic templates of the reported route families, not exact compounds. R labels denote variable positions; they do not imply that every combination is feasible. Amine reagents must have an N–H bond; an R substituent may be H where appropriate. Once a platform is prepared, its installed R groups are fixed for that row or column. A “head” in a diversification panel is the terminal amine choice within its fixed aromatic framework. Source-supported transfer is limited to the demonstrated families.

These are different chemical regions, not alternative routes to identical products. The 2 / 6 / 4 first-compound prices use different core precursors from the same starting pool.

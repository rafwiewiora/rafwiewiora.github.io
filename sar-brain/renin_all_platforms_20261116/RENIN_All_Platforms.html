Coverage and cost assumptions

The 45 retained points are branching/alternative intermediates and assembly joins in the union of frozen cheapest conservative routes, not 45 independent medicinal-chemistry vectors. Platform memberships overlap. The four linear-only compounds are included in their own panel, giving complete coverage of all 173 conservatively priced compounds.

Costs are equal-weight preparation operations from the declared starting stock. Making a compound retains sufficient quantities of every intermediate produced on its recorded route. This retained-material assumption enables cross-platform savings. Quotes stop at any available intermediate; alternative routes are not re-optimized. A multi-operation source record remains one recorded transformation with its original cost.
